Academic Publishing Toolkit for Religious Studies
Purpose Help scholars in religious studies prepare manuscripts for peer reviewed journals in English. Provide checklists, templates, and ready to use statements.
Who it helps Faculty members. Graduate students. Research assistants. Journal editors and reviewers.
What is included Submission checklists. Cover letter and email templates. Response to reviewers templates. Publication ethics statements, conflicts of interest, funding, AI assistance. Basic manuscript templates, structure, and section guidance.
Folder structure checklists email-templates peer-review ethics-and-ai manuscripts journals
How to use Pick the relevant file. Replace placeholders in brackets. Do not include private data in public files.
